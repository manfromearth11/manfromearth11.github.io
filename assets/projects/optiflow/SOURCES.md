# OptiFlow figure sources

All figures are from the authors' arXiv v2 source archive:
https://arxiv.org/src/2609.15883v2 (CC BY 4.0).

- overview.png: rendered from OptiFlow/OptiFlow.pdf with Poppler at 2200px.
- Bandit panels: original PNGs from Bandit_Toy/ (paper Figure 2).
- Four-goal panels: original PNGs from 4way_MDP/ (paper Figure 4).

No scientific figure content has been altered. Benchmark values come from
paper Table 1; the page explicitly identifies the three-method excerpt and
reports Adroit alongside the other domains.

- `algorithm.png`: Algorithm 1, cropped directly from page 6 of the arXiv v2 PDF at 216 DPI; original typesetting and content retained.
